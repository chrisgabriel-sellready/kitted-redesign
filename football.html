Can I use SpinGrip in a game?

Check with your league first. We recommend wearing SpinGrip Football for warm-ups, route trees, footwork drills and 7-on-7 work, every rep from first throw to fully warm.

How do I wear it?

Put your throwing hand's index finger and thumb in an “L” shape, slide the index finger into the top hole and the thumb into the side hole, pull tight on the wrist, and fasten the velcro. Grab a ball and throw normally. When you're good and warm, take it off and keep letting it fly.
