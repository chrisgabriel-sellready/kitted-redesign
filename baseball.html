What the pros say

Got questions?

How does SpinGrip actually work?

SpinGrip is physics. The most optimal way to throw a round object is from the fingers. SpinGrip does this the most optimal way and automatically, without you thinking about anything. Just put on the grip and get to work. SpinGrip's embedded palm-spacer will place the ball where it needs to be in the fingers and do everything for you, so your mind and body can feel what it's like to release the ball the way the best arms on the planet do. It's repeatable and guaranteed to make you more optimal.

Can I use SpinGrip in a game?

Yes, you can use SpinGrip in a game. For pitchers the rules are stricter, so speak with your umpire or league coordinator first. We recommend training with SpinGrip on: bullpens, long toss, flat grounds. Work with it on for every pitch from first throw to fully warmed up, then take the grip off at the end of your session and feel the most optimal you've ever felt.

We have both position players and pitchers using SpinGrip in-game. Every time you throw, warm up, long toss, train defensively or do drill work, you can wear it. It implements the automatic optimal release consistently every time.

We are running active health studies suggesting SpinGrip may alleviate arm stress, which may allow it to be worn in more games in the future. We'll update customers as we learn more.

How do I wear it?

Put your throwing hand's index finger and thumb in an “L” shape, slide the index finger into the top hole and the thumb into the side hole, pull tight on the wrist, and fasten the velcro.

Grab a ball and throw normally.

We recommend wearing the grip from the very first throw and working all the way up to full speed with it on. When you're good and warm, take it off and keep letting it fly. You'll feel a fifth gear every time.

What ages/sizes is SpinGrip available in?

SpinGrip comes in three sizes: Youth (Ages 6-9), Small/Medium (Ages 10-14), and Large/XLarge (Ages 15+). It fits throwing hands of all sizes within these ranges.

Is the data real? How were results measured?

Absolutely. Results were measured using Trackman technology.

When will I start seeing results?

It's common for players to see results immediately. If SpinGrip feels uncomfortable or “not working” at first, that is a clear sign you have been choking the ball and should keep using it to recalibrate your mechanics.

Do results last?

Yes. Because SpinGrip trains the feel of the optimal release, data shows throwing metrics increase with the glove on and continue to improve after taking it off. It imparts a consistent feel of the fingertip release that athletes can own and reproduce as they grow and train.
