SpinGrip gives you this feel and makes it automatic for everyone.

With its embedded palm-spacer, SpinGrip positions the ball into the proper finger placement so every athlete can feel the same release pattern that took the best arms in the game years to develop. You don’t need a coach and you don’t need to think. Just put the grip on and get to work. You will feel the difference immediately.
